# v1.1 Enhancement Proposal — Prioritized Backlog

Produced by the v1.0-completion deep dive (branch `fix/v1.0-completion-and-enhancements`).
Items here were deliberately **not** built in that branch because they are redesigns,
new features, or schema changes. Each entry is scoped so a follow-up agent can build
it directly. Core value yardstick: *students complete Session 1 end-to-end and feel
discovery + completion*.

Already fixed on the branch (for context, not backlog): completion screen wiring +
final persistence (A1), dashboard `id` query (A2), persisted MBTI raw counts (A3),
vitest worktree exclude (A4), undefined `--color-*` CSS variables, discarded
self-map data, explorer-tone narration, class-theme restoration for returning
students, confirmatory header overlap, duplicated progress info, progressbar
semantics, locked-session fallback.

---

## P1 — direct threats to "complete Session 1 and feel it"

### P1.1 Mid-session checkpoint persistence + resume
- **Problem:** All 35+ answers live only in React state. A refresh, tab close, or
  crash at question 30 silently loses everything; the landing page's
  "Continue Quest" button then restarts Session 1 from question 1 with no
  explanation. For a 15–20 minute session with 13–18-year-olds this is the single
  biggest completion killer left.
- **Proposed design:**
  1. Zero-schema first step: serialize `{ questState, scoreState }` to
     `localStorage` (keyed by student id) after every `ANSWER_*` dispatch; on
     session mount, if a snapshot exists and `has_completed_session1` is false,
     show a tone-branched "Welcome back — pick up where you left off?" card with
     Resume / Start over. Add a `RESTORE_STATE` action to `questReducer`.
  2. Optional server step: reuse the (currently dead) checkpoint pattern in
     `providers/quest-provider.tsx` to upsert `session_responses` at block
     boundaries so facilitators see partial progress too.
- **Files:** `hooks/use-quest-state.ts`, `hooks/use-scores.ts`,
  `app/quest/session/[id]/page.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`; step 2 touches
  `providers/quest-provider.tsx` (or a new `lib/persistence/checkpoint.ts`).
- **Risk/effort:** Medium risk (state rehydration must match reducer invariants —
  lock with tests), Medium-Large effort.
- **Why:** Completion rate is the core value; losing 20 minutes of answers is the
  worst possible experience for the target audience.

### P1.2 Unify final persistence with the retry/backoff machinery
- **Problem:** `runFinalPersist` in the session page performs 4 sequential writes
  with no automatic retry; a single transient network blip forces the student to
  notice the banner and press Retry. Meanwhile `providers/quest-provider.tsx`
  contains a complete, tested-looking `retryWithBackoff` + `persistCheckpoint`
  implementation that **nothing imports** (dead code, near-duplicate logic).
- **Proposed design:** Extract one `lib/persistence/final-persist.ts` used by the
  session page: wrap each write in `retryWithBackoff` (1s/2s/4s), keep the
  manual Retry banner as the last resort. Then either delete `QuestProvider` or
  reduce it to a thin re-export — one copy of persistence logic in the codebase.
- **Files:** `app/quest/session/[id]/page.tsx`, `providers/quest-provider.tsx`,
  new `lib/persistence/*`, extend `app/quest/session/__tests__/complete-wiring.test.tsx`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small-Medium effort.
- **Why:** Data written exactly once at the end of the session must survive flaky
  school Wi-Fi.

### P1.3 Make the confirmatory round keep its "charts update live" promise
- **Problem:** The confirmatory intro says "5 quick questions… Your charts will
  update live", but the confirmatory phase renders only a bare `QuestionCard` —
  no charts are on screen, and the student never sees any effect of those 5
  answers. The reward arc deflates right before the finish line.
- **Proposed design:** Either (a) render a compact live mini-chart strip (RIASEC
  bars only) above the confirmatory QuestionCard that animates as scores change,
  or (b) snapshot scores at confirmatory start (`takeSnapshot` in `use-scores.ts`
  already exists and is unused) and show a before/after "Your profile sharpened"
  delta card on the CompletionScreen (e.g. "Investigator +6"). Option (b) is
  cheaper and lands the payoff at the completion moment.
- **Files:** `app/quest/session/[id]/page.tsx`,
  `components/quest/completion-screen.tsx`, `hooks/use-scores.ts` (takeSnapshot
  wiring), tests.
- **Risk/effort:** Low-Medium risk, Medium effort.
- **Why:** This is the "discovery" feeling in its purest form — visible proof
  that answering questions changed the profile.

## P2 — strong experience improvements

### P2.1 Dashboard "How you see yourself vs what we found" card
- **Problem:** Self-map reflections (clarity, sources, perceived strengths) are
  now persisted (fixed on this branch) but displayed nowhere. The comparison
  between self-perception and measured profile is the most interesting insight
  the data can currently support, at zero API cost.
- **Proposed design:** New dashboard card: perceived strengths chips side-by-side
  with detected strengths, matches highlighted ("You called it!"), plus a line
  reflecting the clarity rating ("You started at 2/5 clarity — look how much is
  mapped now"). Pure client-side rendering of existing data.
- **Files:** `app/quest/dashboard/page.tsx`, new
  `components/charts/self-vs-measured.tsx`, test.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Medium effort.
- **Why:** Converts stored data into a felt discovery moment on the dashboard.

### P2.2 Honest XP / reward system
- **Problem:** `XpBar` shows `x / 1000` XP but the maximum earnable in v1 is 450;
  cosmetic thresholds (Background/Accent/Gold Trim) render markers but unlock
  nothing anywhere. `calculateXp` ignores its `currentSession` argument.
  Teenagers notice fake progression instantly.
- **Proposed design:** Scale the bar to the current milestone (e.g. "Chapter 1:
  450/450 XP — Complete!"), make the three cosmetic unlocks real (apply a small
  CSS accent to the dashboard card frame per unlocked tier, stored client-side
  from XP), or drop the markers until Session 2 ships.
- **Files:** `components/ui/xp-bar.tsx`, `app/quest/dashboard/page.tsx`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small-Medium effort.
- **Why:** Trustworthy rewards sustain the completion feeling; fake ones corrode it.

### P2.3 Guard "Start a new quest instead"
- **Problem:** On the landing page, a returning (anonymous-auth) student who taps
  "Start a new quest instead" goes through character creation, which calls
  `signInAnonymously()` again — minting a **new** user and permanently orphaning
  the previous student row, scores, and responses.
- **Proposed design:** In character creation, if a session already exists with a
  student row, show a confirm step ("This replaces your current adventurer —
  your Chapter 1 results will be lost") and on confirm either update the
  existing student row in place (preferred: keep the same auth user, overwrite
  profile, clear scores) or explicitly `signOut()` first.
- **Files:** `app/quest/character/page.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low-Medium risk, Small effort.
- **Why:** Data integrity for minors' assessment data; avoids ghost rows.

### P2.4 Accessible single-control spectrum slider
- **Problem:** `SpectrumSlider` renders three overlapping interaction surfaces
  (clickable track, 7 in-track buttons, 7 fallback buttons below) — screen
  readers announce 14 buttons for one question, and sighted users see two rows
  of controls that do the same thing.
- **Proposed design:** One row of 7 labelled segment buttons inside the track
  (keyboard: arrow keys move selection, Enter confirms), remove the fallback row
  and raw track-click handler; or switch to a real `<input type="range">` styled
  to match (native a11y for free).
- **Files:** `components/quest/spectrum-slider.tsx`, add component test.
- **Risk/effort:** Medium risk (touch behaviour must be re-verified on mobile),
  Medium effort.
- **Why:** MBTI/values answers come through this control; it must be equally
  usable by keyboard, touch, and screen reader.

### P2.5 Instant theme on load (kill the purple flash)
- **Problem:** Class theme is restored only after the Supabase profile fetch
  resolves (fixed on this branch), so magenta/blue students see a purple flash
  of the default theme on every page load.
- **Proposed design:** Cache `avatar_class` + `tone` in `localStorage` at
  character creation and read it in a tiny inline script (or in `ThemeProvider`
  before first paint) to set `data-theme` synchronously; Supabase fetch remains
  the source of truth and corrects drift.
- **Files:** `components/ui/theme-provider.tsx`, `app/quest/character/page.tsx`,
  possibly `app/layout.tsx`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small effort.
- **Why:** The chosen class identity is the student's anchor; flashing someone
  else's colours undermines it.

## P3 — hygiene and later-milestone groundwork

### P3.1 Remove or wire the orphaned theme plumbing
- **Problem:** `ThemeProvider.setTheme` mutates CSS vars but never updates React
  state (context consumers never re-render), and it writes `--theme-*` variables
  that no component reads. Character page bypasses it entirely with a direct
  `setAttribute`.
- **Proposed design:** Slim `ThemeProvider` to: read cached theme, set
  `data-theme`, expose `applyClassTheme` (now in `lib/theme.ts`) — delete the
  `--theme-*` writes and the fake context, or make the context real with state.
- **Files:** `components/ui/theme-provider.tsx`, `lib/theme.ts`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small effort.

### P3.2 Drop unused dependencies
- **Problem:** `recharts` (~large) is a dependency but every chart is custom
  framer-motion markup; `@anthropic-ai/sdk` is installed while all 5 API routes
  are `TODO` stubs. Both are tree-shaken from the client bundle but pollute
  installs, audits, and the mental model.
- **Proposed design:** `npm uninstall recharts`; keep `@anthropic-ai/sdk` only if
  Phase 3 (Claude analysis) starts next milestone, otherwise remove and
  reinstall when needed. Update the stale "Recharts used in dashboard" claim in
  CLAUDE.md's tech-stack section.
- **Files:** `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small effort.

### P3.3 Facilitator experience (currently stubs)
- **Problem:** `app/facilitator/*` pages are 8–14 line placeholders; the RLS
  policies and `facilitator_id` linkage already exist in the schema but there is
  no way to link students or view progress.
- **Proposed design:** v1.1 slice: facilitator sign-in (email auth), a join-code
  mechanism to link students (`students.facilitator_id`), and a roster table
  showing name, class, Session 1 status, and completion date. Read-only; no
  scores detail until consent/story is defined for minors' data.
- **Files:** `app/facilitator/*`, new components, possibly migration for join
  codes.
- **Risk/effort:** Medium risk (auth flows, minors' data privacy), Large effort.

### P3.4 Per-block question framing
- **Problem:** The question card announces "12 of 35" globally — a big number
  that makes the session feel long to younger students.
- **Proposed design:** Frame count per block ("Question 3 of 14 in the Arena of
  Interests") while the thin overall bar keeps global context; the data is
  already computed (`currentBlock.startIndex/endIndex`).
- **Files:** `app/quest/session/[id]/page.tsx`, `components/quest/question-card.tsx`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small effort.

### P3.5 Celebration audio/haptics (opt-in)
- **Problem:** Badge unlock and completion are silent; the reward peak leans
  entirely on visuals.
- **Proposed design:** Small Web-Audio chime + `navigator.vibrate` on badge
  unlock and completion, behind a muted-by-default toggle persisted locally, and
  disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Files:** `components/badges/badge-unlock.tsx`,
  `components/quest/completion-screen.tsx`, small `lib/feedback.ts`.
- **Risk/effort:** Low risk, Small effort.
